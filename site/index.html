      Where the price could go

      Dark line: daily closes. Blue band: the AI's 80% range; gray dashed band: the volatility baseline (no view). Dots mark the stored horizon ranges (1 week, 1 month and so on); the curve between them is a smooth guide that widens with the square root of time, not a separate forecast. Zoom out (scroll or pinch) to see the longer horizons out to 5 years, or use the buttons; drag to pan and hover for exact values. Optional layers: faint bands from earlier forecasts, small squares for dated events (red for shocks; hover to read them), green or red dots where a past forecast resolved inside or outside its range, and candles for the last ~300 sessions.       Wall Street consensus

      Events that can move the market

      Scheduled dates come from official calendars (BLS, Federal Reserve, NYSE). Earnings dates come from Robinhood's calendar and can change. Recent shocks are context only: they do not show that these events predict price moves. Items under Being watched are rumors or unconfirmed reports. Impact ranges are judgment, not scored; META's are SPY's times 1.6, because on two extreme 2025 days META moved about 1.9x and 2.5x SPY's move. The percentage is the model's own estimate, it is judged against what happens, and a rumor can be wrong.
