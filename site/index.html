    Where the price could go

    Dark line: daily closes. Blue band: the AI's 80% range; gray dashed band: the volatility baseline (no view). Dots mark the stored horizon ranges (1 week, 1 month and so on); the curve between them is a smooth guide that widens with the square root of time, not a separate forecast. Zoom out (scroll or pinch) to see the longer horizons out to 5 years, or use the buttons; drag to pan and hover for exact values. Optional layers: faint bands from earlier forecasts, small squares for dated events (red for shocks; hover to read them), green or red dots where a past forecast resolved inside or outside its range, and candles for the last ~300 sessions.
