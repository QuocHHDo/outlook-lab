How scoring works

Coverage is how often the close landed inside the range. An 80% range should cover about 80% over many predictions: far higher means it is too wide to be useful, far lower means it is overconfident. The interval score rewards narrow ranges that still hold (lower is better). Comparisons with the baseline are shown only after enough predictions have resolved.
